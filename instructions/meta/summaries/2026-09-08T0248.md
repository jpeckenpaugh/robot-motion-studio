## What was built

- **Multi-robot management:** create/edit/select/delete robots with independent
  config (name, description, servo inventory, calibration, sim_config);
  backend-persisted `current_robot` state; `?robot_id=` scoping.
- **Door durability seed rig:** wall-mounted arm (shoulder/pivot, elbow, wrist
  twist, gripper end-effector) that grasps a door handle and opens/closes the
  door repeatedly; seeded once, behaves like normal editable data.
- **Timeline editor:** step blocks on a time axis — add/reorder/edit/remove/
  duplicate, play/pause/scrub; per-step pose-or-servo target, duration, hold,
  speed, easing.
- **Advanced timing:** easing curves (linear/ease_in/ease_out/ease_in_out),
  sequence repeat (`repeat_count`/`repeat_forever`), step groups with repeat.
- **Simulator:** browser Canvas renderer + shared `motion.js` (canonical
  easing/speed/loop math) consuming the extended `/preview` as the single motion
  source; export scoped to selected robot, `schema_version` 2.
- v0.1 behavior preserved (no regression).
